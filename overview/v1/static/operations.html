Coverage

Seats claim work orders. Jobs never claim. Supervisor dispatches seats and does not build. Coverage shows hired vs missing providers per project, including idle projects with no open queue. At most 2 implementation seats may run in parallel. State comes from the WorkForce ledger.

Coverage is not a hire-now list — empty-queue projects may stay unstaffed on purpose. Hire… copies a WorkForce command when you want one; it is not For You work. Hiring runs on this Mac through WorkForce; the desk never writes the roster.
